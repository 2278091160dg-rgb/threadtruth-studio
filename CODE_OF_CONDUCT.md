# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in ThreadTruth Studio respectful and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific, constructive, and considerate.
- Critique work and evidence, not people.
- Respect privacy, consent, licensing, and the dignity of image subjects.
- Do not post sexualized content involving minors, non-consensual intimate material, harassment, private data, credentials, or unlicensed customer media.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and avoid misleading claims about affiliation or verification.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports should use the same private channel described in `SECURITY.md`; a concrete contact will be configured before the first public Release.

Enforcement decisions should explain the violated rule while minimizing redistribution of sensitive content.
